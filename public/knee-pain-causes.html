Common Causes of Knee Pain

Knee pain can result from various factors including overuse, injury, or age-related wear and tear.

1. Overuse

Repeated stress from walking, running, or standing can lead to discomfort.

2. Injury

Ligament injuries, meniscus tears, or falls may affect knee stability.

3. Aging

Joint wear and reduced cartilage can contribute to long-term knee pain.
